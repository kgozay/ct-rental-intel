# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Cape Town renters and relocation-minded property seekers comparing residential rentals across selected suburbs. They use the product during an active, often time-sensitive search and need to identify promising listings without repeatedly scanning noisy property portals.

## Product Purpose

Cape Town Rental Intelligence consolidates current rental listings, normalises them, compares price per square metre against local market medians, and exposes the results through filters, comparisons, charts, a map, shortlisting, and AI-assisted analysis. Success means users can quickly understand the market, identify genuinely underpriced homes, and move from broad research to a confident shortlist.

## Brand Personality

Bold, analytical, and direct, with a slightly playful edge. The experience should feel like trustworthy market intelligence made approachable for an individual renter.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic corporate property portals, luxury-estate visual clichés, anonymous blue-and-white SaaS dashboards, and decorative styling that makes the underlying data harder to scan or trust.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with decisions, not raw data.
2. Make every score and recommendation explainable.
3. Preserve the urgency and energy of the Electric & Ink identity without sacrificing scanability.
4. Keep comparison workflows fast, predictable, and easy to recover from.
5. Treat data freshness and data limitations as visible parts of the product.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, color-blind-safe state communication, readable contrast, screen-reader labels, and reduced-motion preferences.
